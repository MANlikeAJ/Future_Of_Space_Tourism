About the conference

Space is becoming a commercial industry

Space is no longer only for government missions and professional astronauts. Our conference brings together space companies, researchers, universities and technology organisations to look at how space travel is becoming a commercial industry.

We will cover the whole passenger journey, from booking, medical screening and training to launch, safety and customer service. We will also talk about the costs and operational limits that stop space tourism from growing.

The passenger journey we will explore

  1. Book

    Choose your journey and reserve your place.

  2. Medical screening

    Make sure every passenger is fit to fly.

  3. Training

    Prepare for launch and for life in space.

  4. Launch

    Launch procedures and passenger safety.

  5. In flight

    Customer service on a journey like no other.

  6. Life in orbit

    Accommodation, dining, entertainment and private facilities.

FAQ

Questions, answered

When and where is the conference?

4 to 8 February 2027 at Dubai Exhibition Centre, Expo City Dubai.

How do I register?

Registration is planned to run until 21 January 2027. Use the form above to register your interest and we will email you as soon as it opens.

How much do tickets cost?

Ticket details will be announced when registration opens.

Can I join online?

Yes. A livestream is part of the plan, and you can register your interest in joining online above. The full list of livestreamed sessions will be confirmed with the programme.

Who can attend?

Industry professionals, researchers, students, investors, press and space enthusiasts are all welcome. Day 1 is a public exposition. Some workshops are closed sessions for invited participants.

Who is speaking?

Keynote speakers, sponsors and partners will be announced once they are confirmed.

How can my organisation get involved?

Sponsorship packages include exposition stalls, session branding and dinner sponsorship. We aim to sign sponsors by 10 December 2026, so please get in touch early using the form above.
